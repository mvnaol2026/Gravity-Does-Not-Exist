I found myself standing not merely on the ruins of the world, but facing its complete disappearance. There was no world left.

I resolved to save the world—to bring it back into existence. I began at its absolute core. I realized that the universe is fundamentally simple: the world is nothing but the motion of matter.

This meant I had to decode only two fundamental concepts:
1. The nature of Motion.

I began analyzing Motion. And once again—sheer shock!

An absent, frozen world. A total catastrophe!

I realized that some underlying mechanism had to keep pushing material bodies forward after the visible external force vanished. That was the exact moment everything fell into place, because I filled the Universe with a continuous medium—the aol space.
